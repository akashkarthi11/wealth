Rewards, with fewer taps

You said it still isn't user friendly and weren't sure which part. These screens are my proposal, based on how the flow works today. Tap the sample screens: they respond. Nothing in the app has changed. Tell me what you like and what you don't, and I'll build only that.

What I think gets in the way today

  • A pop-up before every Accept. It asks "Accept this payment?" each time, then shows a message afterwards. That is two interruptions for the thing you do most.
  • Switches and drop-downs everywhere. Each reward has a switch and a "Received in" drop-down. With two rewards that is four controls before you can decide.
  • Too much explaining. Long sentences inside the yellow block, where one short line would do.
  • The result is hard to find. After mapping, you have to open a row to see where the cashback went.
  • The offer form asks for everything. Account, what you get, where, limits, more: five sections for a "5% at Swiggy" rule.

One reward: nothing to decide

TodaySwitch, drop-down, pop-up confirm, then a message.

ProposedThe reward is one line: what you get and where it goes. Tap Accept and it is done. A short Undo bar replaces the pop-up, so a mistake is one tap to take back.

  • "→ Demo Credit Card" is the receiving account. Tap Change only when you want another.
  • The balance line shows what Accept will do, in plain words.

Needs a new build: Undo has to reverse the balance and the cashback entry. I have the pieces from "change receiving account", but it is new work.

Screen B · two or more match

Yellow, with big tap targets

TodayA list of switches with a long hint, and a drop-down under each.

ProposedEach reward is one big card. Tap to pick, tap again to drop. The main button tells you what will happen: Accept, skip rewards until you pick, then Accept + map 2 rewards. Try it.

  • "Pick all" for the common case where you want everything.
  • The receiving account is a single line under the cards, one choice for all picked rewards. A per-reward choice sits behind "Change".
  • No pop-up. Undo, as in screen A.

Total shown as you pick: it adds the picked rewards in ₹, points shown separately.

Three questions, not five sections

TodayOne long sheet: account, what you get, where it applies, limits, more.

ProposedStart from what you know: pick a kind, answer two or three plain fields, and read the sentence that will be saved. Limits, dates and "ask me" sit under More options, closed.

  • A sentence at the bottom repeats the rule in words, so you can check it before saving.
  • "Received in" is a normal field with a clear default.
