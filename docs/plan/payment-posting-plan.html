Payments should move the balance on their own

ChatGPT saves each payment as a row, but the account balance, the credit card amount owed and the reward points are not updated with it. This plan shows what the app already does (checked in the code on 14 Nov 2026), what is still missing, and the order to fix it.

How it works today

flowchart LR
  A[Gmail payment mail] --> B[ChatGPT hourly task]
  B -->|inserts row, never touches balances| C[(Transactions: pending)]
  U[Upload sheet] --> C
  C --> D{Owner clicks}
  D -->|Accept| E[Source account balance ± amount]
  D -->|Dismiss| F[Row kept, balance unchanged]
  E --> G[Matching offers shown]
  G -->|marked used| H[Offer use saved with expected benefit]
  • ChatGPT is told not to change balances. task.md says “NEVER change balances” and “Never change balances from email”. It only inserts the transaction through recordEmailImport in api/_ingest.js.
  • New rows wait as “pending” (migration 0029; the column default is pending, so ChatGPT rows and uploaded rows both wait). Older rows have no status and are never listed.
  • Accept moves one balance. acceptTransaction in api/_accept.js adds or subtracts the amount on the row’s own account, saved as a “reported” balance. On a credit card or loan the balance is the amount owed, so a purchase raises it.
  • Offers are shown, not credited. recordOfferUses in api/_offers.js saves that an offer was used and its expected benefit in wealth_offer_uses. Nothing is added to a reward balance and no cashback transaction is created.

The fix, in three parts

A. One posting function

Move the money rules out of signedDelta into one function that returns every movement a payment causes: own account, other account and reward. Accept, Upload and any later automatic posting all call it, so the rules live in one place. It posts once per row, so clicking twice cannot double count.

B. A second account on the payment

Add an optional “other account” to each transaction (a new column, so this needs your approval before it is built). ChatGPT fills it for card payments and own-account transfers when the mail names the card or account. At Accept, a “Paid to” dropdown lets you set or fix it. Without it, cases 1 and 2 cannot be right.

C. Rewards at Accept

When you tick an offer as used, the app also writes the reward. Points, coins and monies go in as a reward event “earned” on the program linked to that account (recordRewardEvent already exists). Cashback that arrives as money is recorded as income on the card, as rules.md already says. The Accept screen shows the line to be written before you confirm, for example “Add 120 points to the HDFC Swiggy program”.

What ChatGPT keeps doingOnly inserting the transaction (and the other account when known). It still never writes balances or rewards. The app does that after the rules above, so a forgotten step in the ChatGPT prompt can no longer leave a balance wrong.

What I need you to decide

1. Click or automatic?Keep the Accept click for every row, or post automatically for safe cases (phase 4)? I recommend click first, then automatic for savings once it has run clean for a while.

2. Loan EMIThe data does not say how much of an EMI is interest. Reduce the loan by the full EMI, or ask you for the principal part at Accept?

3. CashbackFor offers that give cashback, record it when the offer is used (expected amount) or only when the money actually arrives and shows in a statement or mail?

4. Older rowsRows from before 14 Nov have no status. Leave them as they are (recommended), or list them once so you can accept them?

Safety rules for the build

  • Every balance written is a new row with who and when; nothing is overwritten. An “undo accept” writes a reversing row.
  • A row is posted once. Accept twice returns “already handled”.
  • A verified balance from a screenshot is never changed by posting; posting only adds a newer “reported” balance.
  • Money amounts are rounded to 2 decimals in one place.
  • No new table or column, and no change to ChatGPT’s permissions, without your approval first.
